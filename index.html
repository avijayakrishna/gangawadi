Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Instant transparent answers regarding global fulfillment, certifications, and trade guidelines.

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

expand_more

MOQs differ based on categories. For Handicrafts (Channapatna toys, Bidriware, Kinnal artworks), we offer flexible small-batch crates for boutique dealers. For agricultural commodities like Coir textiles or Millets, we typically default to 1 Full Container Load (FCL).

Can you supply verified organic millets?

expand_more

Yes, our superfoods and Finger Millets are sourced from certified organic dryland agricultural clusters in Karnataka. We provide complete APEDA-aligned tracking documents and batch certificate numbers representing geographical regions.

Do you offer Private Label and OEM capabilities?

expand_more

Absolutely. We specialize in bespoke private labeling. We handle custom-molded wooden retail-ready toy collections and design high-barrier consumer pouches complete with your exact branding guidelines for international retail launches.

What standard export documentation do you provide?

expand_more

We supply a comprehensive export dossier: Certified Commercial Invoice, detailed Weight & Packing List, Bill of Lading, Registered Certificate of Origin, State Phytosanitary certificates, and specific Cluster GI authentication.

What are typical factory lead times?

expand_more

Handcrafted lines require approximately 4 to 6 weeks, depending strictly on the physical carving and seasoning limits. Agricultural superfoods, on the other hand, can be consolidated, vacuum-packaged, and containerized in 15-20 days.

What are the standard payment protocols?

expand_more

We accommodate 100% Irrevocable Letter of Credit (L/C) at sight from top-tier institutional banks, or a standard 30% advance telegraphic transfer (T/T), with the remainder settled upon dispatch of documents.
