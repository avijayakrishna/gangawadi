Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions from our global importers and wholesale partners.

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?

chevron_right

Our MOQs vary by category: Channapatna Toys , Bidriware ,kinnal art,coirs, and Millets (1 Full Container Load / FCL). Smaller trial orders can be discussed for first-time partners.

Can you supply certified millets?

chevron_right

Yes, all our millets are sourced from certified clusters. We provide certification and GI-tag verification for specific regional varieties.

Do you offer OEM / Private Label packaging?

chevron_right

Absolutely. We offer end-to-end private labeling services including custom box design for handicrafts and retail-ready pouches for agricultural products.

What export documentation do you provide?

chevron_right

We provide a full export set: Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading, Certificate of Origin (CoO), GI Certificates, and Phytosanitary Certificates as required.

What are the standard lead times?

chevron_right

Handicrafts typically require 4-6 weeks depending on order volume. Agricultural products can be dispatched within 15-20 days post-payment confirmation.

What payment terms do you accept?

chevron_right

We primarily work with 100% Irrevocable Letter of Credit (L/C) at sight or 30% Advance T/T with the balance against scanned shipping documents.

Can we appoint independent third-party inspectors?

chevron_right

Yes, we welcome inspections from SGS, Intertek, or Bureau Veritas at our consolidation centers prior to final container sealing.
